ICP Philosophy
The goal is NOT to target "everyone hiring engineers."
The goal is to target organizations where:
hiring mistakes are expensive
engineering quality matters
scaling creates evaluation problems
internal interviewing is inconsistent
technical leadership lacks bandwidth
signal quality is degrading
The strongest ICPs are organizations already experiencing hiring pain.

ICP Priority
PRIORITY 1 — Technical Recruiting Agencies
Why This ICP Matters
This is currently the strongest ICP for founder-led outbound.
These organizations:
already operate inside hiring workflows
already understand hiring pain
already lose money from weak technical validation
often lack deep engineering interviewing capability
need stronger technical credibility with clients
The conversation is easier because the pain already exists.
Common Problems
inability to deeply validate engineers
dependence on client-side interviews
weak technical differentiation
false positives
failed placements
lack of interviewer calibration
inability to assess engineering maturity
Emotional Drivers
protecting client trust
improving placement quality
reducing failed hires
increasing technical credibility
competing with stronger agencies
Best Messaging Angles
Strong:
engineering signal quality
structured technical evaluation
AI-assisted interview distortion
interviewer consistency
false positive reduction
technical calibration
Weak:
generic recruiting support
outsourcing language
staffing augmentation framing
Positioning
NOT:
"external interviewers"
INSTEAD:
technical evaluation partner
engineering signal layer
structured assessment partner
PRIORITY 2 — Small Engineering-Led Startups
Profile
10–50 engineers
founder-led or engineering-led
remote hiring
growing team carefully
no mature interviewing infrastructure
Common Problems
founders still heavily involved in hiring
inconsistent interviewing
weak hiring confidence
overloaded engineering managers
noisy candidate evaluation
Messaging Angles
Strong:
hiring signal degradation
scaling without losing standards
engineering judgment
structured evaluation
production reasoning
PRIORITY 3 — Fractional CTOs / Small Technical Leadership Teams
Why This ICP Works
These people:
deeply understand engineering risk
have limited bandwidth
often need trusted evaluators
already feel hiring pain personally
Common Problems
no structured interview system
interview fatigue
lack of calibrated interviewers
weak technical screening process
ICPs To Avoid Right Now
Large Enterprises
Reasons:
slow sales
procurement complexity
difficult founder-led access
relationship-heavy sales motion
Generic Recruiting Firms
If they:
do not focus on technical hiring
do not understand engineering hiring complexity
optimize only for volume
they are weak-fit ICPs.
Founder-Led Sales Principle
Current GTM should optimize for:
fast conversations
trust-heavy selling
insight-led positioning
operational credibility
NOT:
scale
automation
enterprise complexity
Core GTM Insight
The strongest buyers right now are not companies looking for "interview services."
The strongest buyers are organizations already experiencing:
hiring uncertainty
technical evaluation problems
weak engineering signal
scaling hiring pressure
and already aware that traditional interviewing is becoming less reliable.
