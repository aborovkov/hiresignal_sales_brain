# ICP priority (pinned 2026-07-29)

**Primary target: the owner / partner of a small technical recruiting agency
(~10-40 people), recruiting for engineering roles, who is still hands-on with
screening.** One person where the pain (reads the resumes) and the authority
(owns the budget) overlap.

This resolves the "founders vs hands-on recruiters" tension. It is not one or the
other - it is the overlap, and the two things people mean by "wrong target" are
both about NON-ICP shapes:

- Not hands-off founders (ex-VC, big-company execs). They do not feel the
  screening pain, so a pain-led opener gets a polite "keep in mind for future."
- Not hands-on recruiters inside large companies (e.g. Checkout.com, Deloitte,
  ShipMonk). They feel the pain but do not own the buying decision ("I'm not the
  right person for the decision").

## Hunting cut

Prefer: agency owner / co-founder / partner titles, firm size ~10-40, engineering
or tech recruiting focus. ~~Nordic / UK / EU~~ **ОТМЕНЕНО 2026-08-07 (Alexey): гео любое,
кроме России и Украины - не деприоритизировать по рынку, см. `icp-decision.md`.**
Deprioritize: in-house corporate
recruiters at large employers, and hands-off founders who no longer touch resumes.

**Дополнение 2026-08-07 (Alexey):** фракциональные / фрилансящие / соло технические
рекрутеры - тоже полноценная цель (partner-network fit), и это активный фронт для
хантинга. Наличие своей клиентской базы гейтом НЕ является.
