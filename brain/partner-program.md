# Partner program (HR / TA referral resale)

Captured: 2026-08-17, from Alexey (voice note, details partial).

The offer: HR and talent-acquisition people who have their own clients can
resell StepUP's service - running technical interviews - to those clients.
StepUP shares revenue with the partner for every unit sold.

- Unit = one interview.
- Share: **10% of each interview billed to a referred client** (fixed by Alexey,
  2026-08-17, first quoted to Olga Cipruse).
- Partner's job: the intro only. StepUP runs the sale, the pilot and delivery.
- Client price: NOT quoted in outbound. Depends on volume/stack. Do not name
  the unconfirmed $1,000/mo anchor.
- Entry step for a referred client (Alexey, 2026-08-17, supersedes the resume-batch
  wedge for THIS motion): a **diagnostic meeting**, ideally with the client's own
  engineers, to understand their situation and calibrate the interview to their
  real needs - not a flat, one-size rubric. Pricing talk comes after that meeting.
  (offer.md still lists the free resume-batch wedge as the general entry motion;
  if Alexey confirms the meeting-first flow universally, update offer.md too.)
- Target partner profile: freelance/fractional recruiters, HR consultants,
  agency folks with client books, HR leaders between roles who consult.
- Positioning: lets a partner add a technical-screening arm to their offer
  with zero delivery burden - we run the interviews, they keep the client
  relationship.

First live use: opener to Olga Cipruse (HR leader, open to work, Riga).

## Market read: how recruiters will receive the partner pitch (Sofiia Harasaian, 2026-08-18)

Source: DM thread, crm/sofiia-harasaian.md (HR mentor who trains recruiters - reads
the market for a living). Her unprompted segmentation of recruiter reactions:

1. ~1/3 will fear COMPETITION - "вы придете и заберете клиента".
2. ~1/3 will say it COMPLICATES the process - one more party in the loop.
3. ~1/3 will APPROVE, because we make it FASTER: the client's staff engineers
   have their own workload and little time for interviews.

Implications for the pitch (not yet field-tested):
- Lead with the segment-3 angle: speed, and unloading the client's own engineers.
- Pre-empt segment 1 in the first message: intro-only, the partner keeps the
  client relationship, we never sell past them.
- For segment 2, the answer is the diagnostic-meeting entry step: one meeting,
  we absorb the process burden, the recruiter's flow does not change.
- Her verdict: "идея крутая! Свои клиенты будут, главное правильный маркетинг".
- Also note her second monetization frame from the thread: partner takes 10% OR
  adds their own margin on top of our price ("то что они сверху добавят к цене") -
  Alexey floated both; not yet fixed which is the offer.
