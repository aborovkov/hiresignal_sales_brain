## What the A/B has to beat

Baseline reply rate on the cold-invite motion: **0 of 208**. Baseline on the warm 1st-degree DM
motion: **~13%**. The 49 accepted-invite leads are warm by degree but cold by contact, so the

## Working hypotheses this A/B tests

1. **Reply speed is a qualifier, not a metric.** Every reply ever received landed inside 24h,
   most inside minutes. If a lead is silent for a day, expect nothing. Follow-ups into silence
   have never worked here.
2. **The predictor is "do they personally read CVs", not company type.** Both declines came from
   people who delegate screening (Fernando: "we have our CTO"; Margarita: GetBee builds its own
   AI). All three positives came from people who do it themselves. C_cv_callback asks this
   question directly in the first message.
3. **The standing ICP may be aimed at the wrong seat.** `brain/icp-decision.md` targets recruiting
   agencies, but the single best outcome came from a Head of Engineering who screens personally.

## Measurement rules for the run

- Log every send with `python3 scripts/log_sent.py --variant C|A2` in the same session, including
  people who never answer. No exceptions - this is the whole point.
- Re-run `funnel.py` and `funnel_report.py` against this file to read the delta.
- Judge no earlier than 48h after the last message, given hypothesis 1.
- `wrong_lead` stays Alexey's verdict only; never inferred.
