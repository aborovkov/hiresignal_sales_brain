She asked two things in a row: how video screening works, and whether it is recorded. Coming from

is not idle curiosity. She is checking what happens to candidate data, and behind that sits the

keeps the recording themselves, and uploads only a text file they have chosen to hand over. For a
security-conscious recruiter that is better than an integration, not worse than one. It should be
said in exactly those terms.

"At the moment yoh have to use any notetaker of your choice and just to upload a txt file" frames the
current design as a temporary shortfall, and "integration with notetaker comming little bit later"

recording, we only ever see the text you send us. Also two messages again where one would do, and two
