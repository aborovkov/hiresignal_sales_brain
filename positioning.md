# Core Market Shift

Engineering hiring is becoming noisier.

The main driver is not lack of candidates.

The main driver is signal distortion.

AI-assisted preparation, polished communication, memorized system design patterns, and optimized interview behavior are changing candidate dynamics significantly faster than hiring systems are adapting.

---

# Primary Problem

Most interviewing systems still optimize heavily for:

* interview performance
* prepared explanations
* communication polish
* expected patterns

But engineering work depends on:

* reasoning under ambiguity
* operational judgment
* production tradeoffs
* systems thinking
* debugging capability
* ownership

This creates a growing disconnect between:

* interviewing success
  and
* engineering capability
