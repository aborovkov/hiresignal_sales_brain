## Verbatim quotes

### Homepage

* "The AI-driven recruitment software for agencies."
* Mission framing: make more placements with less admin.
* "Every new job searches for you."
* "Say who you need, out loud if you like."
* Business development: daily market scan for companies likely to hire, produces call lists of warm leads, does **not** send outreach.
* Integrations named: LinkedIn, Gmail, Google Calendar, Xero, job boards, plus an API.
* Slack integration for candidate floats and client chasing, daily dashboard, one-click client responses.
* Talent pool auto-maintenance with job-change and promotion signals, bulk CV import with parsing.
* "No training required for new consultants."

### Pricing

* "$105 per user /mo"
* "One plan with everything Lovelio does, priced per user. No tiers, no modules, no add-ons, no implementation fee, no annual contract."
* "Every user is the same price. No tiers, no step-downs, no surprises."
* "Free to start. No credit card required." Month to month, cancel anytime.
* Included in the single plan: applicant ranking, job ad generation, **phone and video screening**, talent pool, client management, sourcing, business development, API.

### Features - phone screening

* "Lovelio can call and screen all strong candidates automatically. Imagine reviewing an applicant list and they have all been screened, ranked, sorted, and have detailed notes."
* "Intelligent questions generated per candidate" - built from CV, job description and "Client DNA".
* "No scheduling, no interruptions" - candidate joins via an email link.
* "You approve every score" - reviewer sees answers, transcript, detailed notes.

### Features - video screening

* "Video call any client or contact - built in and native. Lovelio prompts you to ask questions, probe more, drill in."
* Live transcript with speaker identification.
* "The write-up does itself" - summary, strengths, watch-outs, salary, notice period auto-populated.
* "Interview to client inbox in minutes."
* Consent-first recording, "audio is kept and auto-deleted on schedule".

### Features - scoring

* Candidates scored on "two axes" during triage.
* Configurable "score thresholds" for advancing or parking candidates.
* Human approval before action.
* WhatsApp and Slack native for pipeline and confirmations.

## Assessment carried into the battlecard

Overlap is real on two surfaces (automated phone screening, recruiter-led video screening with live prompting) but shallow: questions are generated per candidate from CV and JD, with no evidence of a question bank, level-discriminating design or authored rubrics. The competitive pressure is **commercial, not technical** - screening bundled into a $105 flat plan makes standalone evaluation look like an extra line item. Defensible ground is technical roles where a false positive is expensive.
