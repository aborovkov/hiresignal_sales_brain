# PILLARS.md

> The content discipline. Governs what we post and in what mix. Voice is owned by `brain/voice.md`; this file owns **structure**. Speaks the vocabulary of the `linkedin-content-planner` + `linkedin-post-writer` skills so the numbers in `post_log.md` are legible.

## The 3 pillars (mix per week)

| Pillar | Target share | What it is |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Authority** | 40–50% | Proof, receipts, contrarian takes. The "we know hiring signal" layer. |
| **Personal Narrative** | 30–40% | Founder reflection, voice reset, why-we-do-this. Builds trust. |
| **Community** | 20–30% | Polls, spotlights, questions. Pulls the audience in. |
| **Product/Offer** | ≤10–15% | The wedge/offer. **Max 1 post/week.** Overuse kills trust. |

> Pains/signals themes below are **Authority topics, not pillars.** A pillar is a role in the mix; a topic is what a given Authority post is about.

### Authority topic bank (from `brain/pains.md` + `brain/signals.md`)

* False positives — hidden cost of interview-optimized hires.
* Interviewer calibration — why two interviewers reach opposite conclusions.
* AI-era interview distortion — polished prep vs. real engineering signal.

* Weak signal — noise mistaken for competence.
* Structured evaluation — repeatable scoring vs. gut feel.

## Cadence rules (from `linkedin-content-planner`)

* **3–5 posts/week.** 3 pillars minimum, 5 maximum.
* **One format per pillar per week** — vary text / carousel / poll; don't stack 3 text Authority posts.
* **10–20 substantive comments/day** on other creators. Comments drive more inbound than posts.
* **No pillar >60%** of the week's posts.
* **Post window: Tue–Thu, 07:30–09:00 Tbilisi.** Avoid Fri afternoon, Sat/Sun (B2B reach cut 30–50%).
* North star = **inbound**, not reactions. See `post_log.md`.
