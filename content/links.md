### Upwage - agentic AI interviewer for high-volume hourly hiring

- Added: 2026-08-13 (flagged by Alexey)
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/upwageofficial/
- Site: https://www.upwage.com/ | Crunchbase: https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/upwage
- What: agentic AI that runs behavioral interviews 24/7 in any language (STAR methodology); an "AI Analyst" turns transcripts into consistent scorecards. Positioned as "AI Interviews with Proven ROI". Founded 2022, CEO Diana Tsai (Forbes 30 Under 30).
- ICP: high-volume hourly hiring - retail, hospitality, logistics. Named clients: Gopuff, REI, PRN.
- Funding: $9.2M seed, announced 2026-03-23. Investors include AlleyCorp, BBG Ventures, Bloomberg Beta, Core Innovation Capital, J4.Ventures (~10 total). They are funded and will push hard on category ownership.
- Claimed metrics: turnover cut by up to 48%; 77% interview completion rate; candidate NPS 9.01; 4,000-7,000+ custom AI agents deployed; "doubled recruiter productivity".
- Distribution: works standalone OR inside an existing ATS, plus a Chrome extension to view/sort candidate results directly in the ATS.
- Pricing: not published. Market benchmark for enterprise AI video interviews is ~$10-25 per candidate - useful anchor for our own pricing test (C1/C2 in `brain/hypotheses.md`).

**Overlap with StepUP / HireSignal:**
- Async AI interview + auto-scorecard is the same mechanic as our group D bet (claim-validation interview engine, `brain/hypotheses.md`). They validate behavior (STAR), we validate technical claims - different axis, same budget line.
- Their standalone-or-ATS + Chrome-extension distribution is a live data point for C3 (ATS integration as blocker): they chose "both", not either/or.

**Differentiation to hold:**
- Segment: they are hourly/volume non-technical; we are engineering hiring, evidence-based technical validation. Little direct collision today, but "AI interviewer" category framing will blur it for buyers.
- They sell retention ROI (turnover); we sell engineer-time saved + authenticity/fake-detection. Fake-profile problem is not in their pitch at all.
- Watch: if they move upmarket into technical screening, the "proven ROI + named logos" story beats our proof.md as it stands today (proof.md is still TODO).

### AI-интервьюеры: ландшафт категории (обзорно)

- Added: 2026-08-13, при разборе направления «AI-интервьюер» (`brain/integrations.md`)
- Категория уже плотная. Заметные игроки: **Alex AI (ex-Apriora)** - двусторонний голосовой/видео интервьюер, заявляет 5,000+ интервью в день против 16 у человека, 48% вне рабочих часов; **HeyMilo** - end-to-end AI-рекрутер, 20+ языков; **Ribbon AI** - voice-first, 10+ языков, Fast Company Most Innovative 2026; **micro1 (Zara)** - автономный агент плюс сеть проверенных кандидатов; **ConverzAI**, **Sapia.ai**, **Tenzo**, **Tezi**, **HireVue**.
- ⚠️ **Ближе всех к нам: Talently AI** - живые AI-интервью плюс coding assessment именно под технический найм. Это не соседний сегмент, а наше поле; при заходе в интервьюер разбирать первым.
- Что это значит: «AI-интервьюер» как таковой уже не дифференциатор и не выигрывает по новизне. Наш заявленный отличитель - верификация конкретных claim'ов из CV и ось Authenticity Risk (группы D и E в `../brain/hypotheses.md`), а не факт наличия AI-интервью.
- **Willo** (`willo.video`) в этом списке стоит особняком: async-интервью с открытым API и нативными интеграциями (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Oracle). Скорее потенциальный слой под нами, чем конкурент - разбор в `../brain/integrations.md` §6.
