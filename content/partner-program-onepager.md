**Earn 10% of every technical interview we run for a client you introduce.

## Who we are
StepUP is a technical evaluation company. We are not recruiters - we run
technical interviews as a service. Practicing engineers interview your client's
candidates, and the client gets a verifiable, structured read on real
engineering capability. The team is led by an interviewer who has personally
run 1,700+ technical interviews across 28 countries and calibrated interviewer
teams. Our principle: we don't score answers, we score how a candidate thinks.

## The problem we take off your client's plate
In most companies, technical interviews are run by the client's own staff
engineers. Those engineers have a roadmap to ship. Interviews get postponed,
candidates wait, offers slip, and your placement slows down with them. We
remove that bottleneck: our engineers take the interviews over, and the
client's team gets its hours back.

## What the client receives
Every interview runs in Zoom and is recorded, with candidate consent handled
by us. The client receives a copy of the recording plus a structured report on
each candidate - so every conclusion we make can be checked against the
recording, minute by minute. Interviews are calibrated to the client's actual
stack and seniority bar, not a generic template. Calibration happens in a free
diagnostic meeting with the client's team before anything is billed.

## How the program works
1. You name a client where staff engineers run tech interviews and it hurts.
2. You forward our short intro text - we give it to you ready to send.
3. We hold a free diagnostic meeting with their team and calibrate the
   interview to their stack.
4. Our engineers run the interviews and deliver recordings and reports.
5. You receive 10% of every interview we run for that client, paid monthly,
   for as long as they work with us - with a transparent per-interview report,
   so you always see what your cut is based on.

## What we will never do
We sell no sourcing, no placements, no recruiting services of any kind - so
there is nothing for us to compete with you on. The client relationship stays
yours: any expansion of our work with a client you introduced is discussed
through you, not around you.

## Common questions
**Will you end up taking my client?** We can't. Recruiting is not in our
service list, and your revenue share is our reason to keep you in the loop -
your intro is how we grow.

**Does this add complexity to my process?** No step of your process changes.
For you, the whole program is one forwarded message. For the client, the same
interview slot in the calendar is simply taken by our engineer instead of
theirs.

**What does it cost my client?** Pricing depends on volume and stack, and is
agreed after the free diagnostic meeting - the client commits to nothing
before seeing exactly how we'd run their interviews.
