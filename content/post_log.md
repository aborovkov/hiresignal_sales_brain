# POST_LOG.md

> Canonical performance rollup. **One normalized row per published post.** Raw/messy capture goes to `stats_raw/` first, then gets normalized here.
>
> **North star = inbound**, not reactions. A high-reaction post that drives zero DMs/replies is a miss. Weekly: promote what drove inbound back into `brain/` + `pillars.md`.

Column keys:
* **Hook** = formula code `F1`–`F10` (from `linkedin-post-writer`).
* **Pillar** = Authority | Narrative | Community | Product.
* **Format** = text | carousel | poll.
* **Two checkpoints** per post: measure at **T+48h** (early velocity) and **T+14d** (durable reach + late inbound).
