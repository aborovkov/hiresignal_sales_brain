## 2026-07-15 - standard volume-question opener - 3 replies in ~24h, 1 demo booked

Context: first-touch DM, approved structure (volume question -> Turing credential -> volume-based free offer -> "you keep the conversations" close), sent cold to 1st degree connections, batch of 2026-07-15 evening.

Results:

- UK fractional technical recruiter (startups/scale-ups): "I would like to try" in 11 minutes, demo call booked within 16 minutes of first touch. See `../crm/accounts/dina-veprikova.md`.
- In-house TA leader: replied same evening (~4h), explicit pain recognition ("great value add for TA professionals dealing with volume"), promised to try the product. See `../crm/accounts/sean-hassell.md`.
- Agency-side recruiter: declined next day (builds own AI in-house) but unusually warm for a decline, door left open in her own words ("let's stay in touch"). See `../crm/accounts/margarita-isadjanean.md`.

Why it likely worked: the opener leads with a question about their reality instead of a pitch, and the free offer is framed as volume-based (expires on use, not calendar) - low pressure, easy to say yes or decline politely. Even the decline preserved the relationship.

Watch: none of the three replies actually answered the CV-count question - the question earns the reply but does not collect the number; re-ask it on the call / in FU1.
### 2026-07-16 - Roman Ostroushko, Head of Engineering - won: trial acceptance
Context: First-touch DM to a 1st-degree connection. Non-standard structure - role-specific opener instead of the Turing credential.
> Roman, as Head of Engineering - how much of your team's week goes to screening resumes and first-round interviews? We built an evaluation layer that scores how a candidate thinks, not just the answer. I can set you up with 100 resume screenings + 5 AI interviews to try, free, expires on use not calendar. You keep the conversations, we handle the reading.
Why it likely worked: named his exact role and the exact weekly pain, one crisp positioning line ("scores how a candidate thinks, not just the answer"), volume-based free offer with no calendar pressure, and a memorable close ("you keep the conversations, we handle the reading"). No credential dump, no CTA to book a call.

## 2026-07-07 - pain-mirror pitch + concrete test-run offer - agency recruiter: call booked + trial invite + referral in 26h

Context: cold DM to an agency recruiter (IT/FinTech desk, EU-based), 1st degree. Non-standard structure: opener named their agency and geography, used the Turing credential, asked "где основная боль?" instead of pitching. See `../crm/accounts/vitalii-pustobaev.md` for the verbatim thread.

Structure that won (two-move):

1. **Pain mirror:** they named three pains unprompted; the reply mapped the product to each pain point-by-point, in their vocabulary, closing each with the operational payoff ("скор, который можно защитить перед заказчиком", "меньше историй - на интервью зашёл, а клиент завернул").
2. **Collapse into a bounded test run:** "Дайте одну реальную вакансию, я за 24 часа бесплатно прогоню под неё до 10 кандидатов и верну скоркарты с обоснованием по каждому. Посмотрите на своём материале, совпадает ли с вашей интуицией. Идёт?"

Results, within 26 hours of first touch: named a live vacancy for the run, self-booked a discovery call from a Calendly link, requested the platform link, accepted an invite, committed to post-test feedback, and referred a colleague ("Я коллегам идею донес") who agreed to talk.

Why it likely worked: "объяснять дольше, чем показать" flips the pitch into an offer to be judged on their own material - same mechanism as the "your own CV" asset below, but aimed at a live vacancy. The 24h/10-candidates bound makes the yes cheap and the deliverable concrete. Candidate wedge parameters for `../brain/offer.md` (currently TODO): one real vacancy, up to 10 candidates, 24h turnaround, scorecards with per-candidate rationale.
