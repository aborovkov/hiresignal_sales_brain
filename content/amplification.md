# AMPLIFICATION.md

> How one piece of news lives for 2-3 weeks instead of one day. Owns the **distribution cycle**; structure of the mix is in `pillars.md`, voice in `brain/voice.md`. Added 2026-08-21.

## The pattern: 1 fact x 4 forms x 4 audiences, spread over time

A news item is never published once. It passes four stages. At each stage only one thing changes: who is speaking and to whom. Each stage uses the previous one as proof, and each next stage is wider in reach and further from us as the source. By the end the event looks like "everyone was writing about this".

| # | Stage | When | Channel | Who -> whom | Action |
|---|-------|------|---------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | **Source** | day 0 | own LinkedIn | us -> own network | Publish the fact. Goal is not reach but a canonical link to point at later. "The registry entry": now the news exists. |
| 2 | **Hijack** | days 1-4 | comments under 5-10 adjacent posts, threads, chats | us -> other people's audiences | "By the way, we just ..." + link to stage 1. The news arrives from several directions; readers decode that as "people are talking about this". |
| 3 | **Reframe** | week 2 | own LinkedIn / newsletter | us as expert -> ICP | Same news as "what this means for business / market / you". Fact -> thesis -> opinion -> expertise. Different genre, so not a repeat. Must carry one quotable number. |
| 4 | **Hand-off** | week 2-3 | podcast, column, media, partner channel | third party -> their audience | Pitch a ready package (source link + visible thread + analysis), not a press release. Someone else's voice buys independence. |

## Rules

* Stage 2 targets are posts by people in or near the ICP (`brain/icp.md`). Run through `linkedin-comment-drafter`.
* Stage 3 changes the genre, not the wording: story -> lesson, milestone -> metric, feature -> market shift.
* Stage 4 needs a named target and the package assembled before the pitch.
* Log the cycle in `post_log.md` so stage 3 does not collide with next week's source post.
* Default: every Authority or Personal Narrative post from `pillars.md` gets a stage 2-4 plan at draft time.

## Worked example (Aug 2026)

1. Fri 2026-08-21: personal post "first job at 19, 39 now" (20 yrs shipping, 4 yrs freelance, 6 yrs inside a startup that became a unicorn, then StepUP).
2. Sat-Mon: comments under posts about freelancing, burnout, career switches.
3. Week of 08-24: "what 20 years of shipping taught me about how agencies should evaluate engineers", one number.
4. Week of 08-31: pitch the story to a recruiting-industry podcast or column.
