# Outreach cadence and anti-automation SOP

Standing decision, effective 2026-08-02. This is the operational rule sales-brain
and any batch outreach must follow. It exists because a late-July batch hurt reply
rate and account health, and to stop it from happening again.

## What went wrong (the pattern we are banning)

- Near-identical follow-ups sent to dozens of people ("... no bump - just a concrete
  offer. Send me one li..."), word-for-word the same across contacts.
- Those bumps went out about one day after the first DM, in one block, to a whole list.
- Effect: replies read at ~4% on a 4-day-old campaign, and mass-identical fast bumps
  are the exact fingerprint LinkedIn throttles.

## The rules now (hard)

Time floor and effort floor - both must hold before any follow-up sends.

1. First follow-up: not before +3 full days after the first DM (target +3-4 days).
   Must bring a new angle or a new asset. Never "just bumping", never "no bump but ...",
   never "any thoughts?".
2. Second follow-up: +7 days after FU1. Shorter, with an easy face-saving out.
3. After FU2 with no reply: park with a +60 day revisit. No third cold bump unless a
   live trigger appears (vacancy spike, relevant post, mutual intro).
4. The clock is per person, from that person's last outbound touch - never from a batch
   date. Messaged today = FU1 due in 3+ days, whatever the rest of the batch does.
5. No duplicate text across people in one pass. Shared SHAPE is fine (the approved
   opener), but each person's wording must differ - a real personalization line or a
   genuinely different value angle. No real thing to say = they wait, not a filler bump.
6. A follow-up must add, not repeat: a new pain/angle, a new asset, or a real reference
   to something they did. Re-asking the same ask does not send.
7. Batch pacing for account health: default cap ~20-25 fresh first DMs/day and ~15-20
   follow-ups/day, spread across the day. Bigger asks get split across days, and we say so.
8. One message per person per pass. Never queue a first DM and its bump together.
9. Batch request = per-person plan (who is actually due by the 3-day floor, and a distinct
   angle each), surfaced for approval, with not-yet-due people held back.

If a single draft breaks a rule above, drop it from the send and say why (too soon,
nothing new, or would duplicate another person's text) - do not soften and send anyway.

## Стандартное правило от 2026-08-06 (Алексей): борд в каждое первое касание.

Каждый первый DM обязан нести ссылку на живой hiring board. Показываем выход, а не
описываем его. Это же правило распространили на фоллоапы в батче 06.08.

Порядок в тексте: вопрос про их объём или боль -> одна строка ценности -> ссылка на
борд как доказательство -> бесплатный оффер и мягкое закрытие. Для технических
покупателей (CTO, VP Eng) борд ставится в середину как иллюстрация уже сказанного
тезиса, а закрывает по-прежнему вопрос, иначе письмо превращается в оффер и теряет
регистр разговора на равных.

Ссылка не заменяет персонализацию: формулировки у разных людей всё равно обязаны
различаться (правило 5 выше). Общим может быть только сам линк.

Борды генерирует Алексей, Claude их не выдумывает и не подставляет по памяти. Нет
ссылки на руках - спросить, а не отправлять текст без неё.
