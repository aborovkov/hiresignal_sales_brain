# SALES_PROCESS.md

> Process checklist for HireSignal's founder-led motion. Adapted from a generic sales-101 doc — reframed around our actual ICP, wedge, and voice.
> This is the *how-to-run-it* layer. The *what-to-say* lives in `brain/` (`positioning.md`, `voice.md`, `outreach.md`, `objections.md`).

---

# What we're selling

A sale is solving a problem. Keep in mind:

* You sell to several stakeholders at once (financial buyer / technical buyer / influencer / internal coach).
* The deal cycle is multi-stage; leads drop off at each stage (mirror stages in `crm/pipeline.md`).
* We do **not** close on the first call. First contact is for recognition and trust, not conversion (see `brain/outreach.md`).

---

# ICP — know the buyer

Full ICP in `brain/icp.md`. Priority order: (1) technical recruiting agencies, (2) small engineering-led startups, (3) fractional CTOs / small technical leadership teams. For each: what pains do they have, how/when do they decide, and how do they currently solve the problem?

Stakeholders:

* **Financial buyer** — controls budget.
* **Technical buyer** — evaluates whether the signal is real.
* **Influencer** — shapes the decision without owning it.
* **Coach** — internal ally who guides you through.

---

# USP — short and concrete

Strong positioning is short and concrete (see `brain/positioning.md` → "What We Actually Sell"). We deliver: confidence in engineering evaluation, stronger hiring signal, calibration consistency, reduction of false positives. Not tooling, not interviewers, not recruiting.

The value we deliver: **de-risks expensive hiring mistakes** and **makes hiring signal reliable under AI-era distortion.**

---

# Channels & lead list

Primary channel: **LinkedIn (founder-led)**, warm and insight-first. For each ICP, understand where they look for a solution today and how they decide, then reach them there. Build the list from LinkedIn, ATS-integration signals (e.g. Teamtailor users), and referrals. Track everything in the Sheet + `crm/accounts/`.

---

# Composing the message

Combine ICP + USP into a per-ICP message. Use the opener patterns in `brain/outreach.md`. Review each draft against:

* Show value immediately.
* Personalize to the lead.
* Keep it short.
* Don't talk about yourself.
* Don't use a lot of links.
* Be an understandable solution.

Ideal structure: hook → a fact tied to the lead → their pains/risks → how we address them → soft proof → soft CTA. (For HireSignal this is softer than classic SDR — see `voice.md` CTA rules.)

---

# The first call

You will not close it. Its purpose: get to know the lead (BANT — budget, authority, need, timeline), learn how best to present, and — if there's time — show the wedge.

How to run it:

1. Small talk → agenda.
2. Why did the lead take the call?
3. Learn their pains (BANT lives here).
4. Identify which pains we close.
5. Show the free-sample wedge (see `brain/offer.md`).
6. Pricing (only if trust exists).
7. Next steps.

Standard mistakes to avoid: monologuing, over-talking about yourself/company, not asking questions, features instead of value, missing personal pains, no examples/use cases, reusing the same demo for every lead.

Prepare: per-ICP problems, the values that matter to them, the capability that closes those problems, a standing question list, the "moments of excitement," and the pricing policy.

---

# The moment of excitement (three-step)

1. "First, let me show you **A**, because **B**." → e.g. "Let me show you the signal read on 10 of your own resumes, because you mentioned you can't tell polished prep from real depth."
2. "You mentioned task **V** (from B). Do you think **A** closes it?"
3. "What other problems do you think **A + A1 + A2** could solve for you?"

Tie the demo to *their* stated pain, never a canned walkthrough.
